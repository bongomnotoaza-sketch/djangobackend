# Multiplication of Binomials and Trinomials & Simplification

## Multiplication Types

---

# Single-Binomial (SB)

A single term multiplied by a binomial.

$$
a(b+c)
$$

Multiply the term outside the bracket by every term inside.

$$
a \times b+a \times c
$$

### Example

Simplify:

$$
3(x+4)
$$

Multiply:

$$
3(x)+3(4)
$$

$$
3x+12
$$

---

# Single-Trinomial (ST)

A single term multiplied by a trinomial.

$$
a(b+c+d)
$$

Multiply the term outside the bracket by every term inside.

$$
a \times b+a \times c+a \times d
$$

### Example

Simplify:

$$
2(3x^2+5x-4)
$$

Multiply:

$$
2(3x^2)+2(5x)+2(-4)
$$

$$
6x^2+10x-8
$$

---

# Binomial-Binomial (BB)

Two binomials multiplied together.

$$
(a+b)(c+d)
$$

Multiply every term from the first bracket by every term in the second bracket.

$$
ac+ad+bc+bd
$$

### Example

Simplify:

$$
(x+3)(x+5)
$$

Multiply:

$$
x(x)+x(5)+3(x)+3(5)
$$

$$
x^2+5x+3x+15
$$

Now simplify using the family method.

## Step 1: Identify families

Family list:

1. \(x^2\)
2. \(x\)
3. Constants

## Step 2: Label every term

$$
(1)\ x^2+(2)\ 5x+(2)\ 3x+(3)\ 15
$$

## Step 3: Combine families

$$
(1)\ x^2+(2)\ (5+3)x+(3)\ 15
$$

## Step 4: Calculate each family

$$
x^2+8x+15
$$

---

# Binomial-Trinomial (BT)

A binomial multiplied by a trinomial.

$$
(a+b)(c+d+e)
$$

Multiply every term in the first bracket by every term in the second bracket.

$$
ac+ad+ae+bc+bd+be
$$

### Example

Simplify:

$$
(x+2)(x^2+3x+4)
$$

Multiply:

$$
x(x^2)+x(3x)+x(4)+2(x^2)+2(3x)+2(4)
$$

$$
x^3+3x^2+4x+2x^2+6x+8
$$

Now simplify using the family method.

## Step 1: Identify families

Family list:

1. \(x^3\)
2. \(x^2\)
3. \(x\)
4. Constants

## Step 2: Label every term

$$
(1)\ x^3+(2)\ 3x^2+(3)\ 4x+(2)\ 2x^2+(3)\ 6x+(4)\ 8
$$

## Step 3: Combine families

$$
(1)\ x^3+(2)\ (3+2)x^2+(3)\ (4+6)x+(4)\ 8
$$

## Step 4: Calculate each family

$$
x^3+5x^2+10x+8
$$

---

# Powers of Binomials

When a binomial has a power, expand the brackets first.

Example:

$$
(x+2)^3
$$

Rewrite:

$$
(x+2)(x+2)(x+2)
$$

---

## Step 1: Complete BB multiplication

First two brackets:

$$
(x+2)(x+2)
$$

Multiply:

$$
x(x)+x(2)+2(x)+2(2)
$$

$$
x^2+2x+2x+4
$$

Simplify:

$$
x^2+4x+4
$$

---

## Step 2: Complete BT multiplication

Multiply:

$$
(x^2+4x+4)(x+2)
$$

$$
x^2(x)+x^2(2)+4x(x)+4x(2)+4(x)+4(2)
$$

$$
x^3+2x^2+4x^2+8x+4x+8
$$

---

## Step 3: Simplify using families

Family list:

1. \(x^3\)
2. \(x^2\)
3. \(x\)
4. Constants

Label:

$$
(1)\ x^3+(2)\ 2x^2+(2)\ 4x^2+(3)\ 8x+(3)\ 4x+(4)\ 8
$$

Combine:

$$
(1)\ x^3+(2)\ (2+4)x^2+(3)\ (8+4)x+(4)\ 8
$$

Final answer:

$$
x^3+6x^2+12x+8
$$

---

# Simplification Rules

## Like Terms

Like terms have:

* The same variables
* The same powers

Only like terms can be combined.

Example:

$$
3x+5x=8x
$$

$$
4x^2+7x^2=11x^2
$$

Cannot simplify:

$$
3x+5x^2
$$

because \(x\) and \(x^2\) are different families.

---

# Family Method For Simplification

## Step 1: Identify Families

Create a family list.

Example:

$$
8x^2+6x+20x+15
$$

Family list:

1. \(x^2\)
2. \(x\)
3. Constants

---

## Step 2: Label Every Term

$$
(1)\ 8x^2+(2)\ 6x+(2)\ 20x+(3)\ 15
$$

---

## Step 3: Combine Families

$$
(1)\ 8x^2+(2)\ (6+20)x+(3)\ 15
$$

---

## Step 4: Calculate Families

$$
8x^2+26x+15
$$

---

# Sign Rules

Signs belong to each term.

$$
(+)\times(+)=+
$$

$$
(+)\times(-)=-
$$

$$
(-)\times(+)=-
$$

$$
(-)\times(-)=+
$$

Example:

$$
(3x-5)(2x+4)
$$

Multiply:

$$
3x(2x)+3x(4)-5(2x)-5(4)
$$

$$
6x^2+12x-10x-20
$$

Simplify.

Family list:

1. \(x^2\)
2. \(x\)
3. Constants

Label:

$$
(1)\ 6x^2+(2)\ 12x+(2)\ -10x+(3)\ -20
$$

Combine:

$$
(1)\ 6x^2+(2)\ (12-10)x+(3)\ -20
$$

Answer:

$$
6x^2+2x-20
$$

---

# Multiplying Coefficients and Variables

Multiply numbers first, then variables.

Example:

$$
3x \times 4x
$$

Numbers:

$$
3\times4=12
$$

Variables:

$$
x\times x=x^2
$$

Answer:

$$
12x^2
$$

---

# Multiplying Variables With Powers

When multiplying the same variables, add powers.

Rule:

$$
x^a\times x^b=x^{a+b}
$$

Example:

$$
x^2\times x^3=x^5
$$

---

# Zero Families

A family can disappear when it becomes zero.

Example:

$$
5x^2+3x-3x+4
$$

Family list:

1. \(x^2\)
2. \(x\)
3. Constants

Label:

$$
(1)\ 5x^2+(2)\ 3x+(2)\ -3x+(3)\ 4
$$

Combine:

$$
(1)\ 5x^2+(2)\ (3-3)x+(3)\ 4
$$

Since:

$$
3-3=0
$$

Final answer:

$$
5x^2+4
$$

---

# General Simplification Method

## Step 1

Classify all multiplication:

* BB
* BT
* SB
* ST

If there are no brackets, move directly to simplification.

---

## Step 2

Complete multiplication and simplify using the family method.

---

## Step 3

Apply the sign outside a bracket to every term inside.

Example:

$$
-(x+3)
$$

becomes:

$$
-x-3
$$

---

## Step 4

Remove brackets and run the family simplification method again.

---

# Multiplication Order Rule

Always complete multiplication in this order:

1. BB
2. BT
3. SB or ST

Example:

$$
5x(4y-6)^2
$$

Contains:

* BB: \((4y-6)^2\)
* SB: \(5x(\text{answer})\)

First complete BB.

---

## Step 1: Complete BB

$$
(4y-6)(4y-6)
$$

Multiply:

$$
4y(4y)+4y(-6)-6(4y)-6(-6)
$$

$$
16y^2-24y-24y+36
$$

Simplify using families:

Family list:

1. \(y^2\)
2. \(y\)
3. Constants

Label:

$$
(1)\ 16y^2+(2)\ -24y+(2)\ -24y+(3)\ 36
$$

Combine:

$$
(1)\ 16y^2+(2)\ (-24-24)y+(3)\ 36
$$

Answer:

$$
16y^2-48y+36
$$

---

## Step 2: Complete SB

$$
5x(16y^2-48y+36)
$$

Multiply:

$$
80xy^2-240xy+180x
$$

Final answer:

$$
80xy^2-240xy+180x
$$

---

This is now the complete method format: **every multiplication is expanded, every term receives a family number, and every simplification is traceable.**
