# Final Exam Tips: Algebra and Equations

Algebra questions are not only about getting the final answer. Marks are awarded for the **method**, so always show your working clearly.

The goal is:

$$
\textbf{Identify the type of question → Apply the correct method → Show every step → Check your answer}
$$

---

# 1. General Algebra Rules

## Always Write Every Step

Do not jump from:

$$
2x+5=15
$$

straight to:

$$
x=5
$$

Show:

$$
2x+5-5=15-5
$$

$$
2x=10
$$

$$
x=5
$$

Every line shows your method.

---

## Keep Signs Attached to Terms

A negative sign belongs to the number or term after it.

Example:

$$
5x-3x
$$

means:

$$
5x+(-3x)
$$

When multiplying:

$$
(x-4)(x+3)
$$

Treat it as:

$$
x(x)+x(3)-4(x)-4(3)
$$

---

# 2. Simplification Tips

Before simplifying:

## Step 1

Identify families.

Example:

$$
5x^2+3x+7x^2+4x+8
$$

Family list:

1. \(x^2\)
2. \(x\)
3. Constants

---

## Step 2

Label every term.

$$
(1)5x^2+(2)3x+(1)7x^2+(2)4x+(3)8
$$

---

## Step 3

Combine only matching families.

$$
(1)(5+7)x^2+(2)(3+4)x+(3)8
$$

---

Remember:

$$
x \neq x^2
$$

They are different families.

---

# 3. Multiplication Tips

Before multiplying brackets:

## Identify the type

Ask:

Is it:

* SB?
* ST?
* BB?
* BT?

---

## Multiplication Order

Always follow:

1. BB
2. BT
3. SB/ST

Example:

$$
5x(4y-6)^2
$$

First:

$$
(4y-6)^2
$$

Then:

$$
5x(\text{answer})
$$

---

## Do Not Forget Any Pair

For BB:

$$
(a+b)(c+d)
$$

There are four multiplications:

$$
ac+ad+bc+bd
$$

Missing one term causes the whole answer to be wrong.

---

# 4. Factorisation Tips

Always check methods in this order:

## Step 1

Common factor first.

Example:

$$
2x^2-18
$$

Do not start with difference of squares.

First:

$$
2(x^2-9)
$$

Then:

$$
2(x+3)(x-3)
$$

---

## Step 2

Look for patterns.

Difference of squares:

$$
a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)
$$

---

## Step 3

For trinomials:

Check:

Multiply:

$$
a \times c
$$

Add:

$$
b
$$

---

# 5. Solving Equations Tips

## Linear Equations

Remember the goal:

$$
\text{Variable alone}
$$

Do the opposite operation.

Example:

$$
+5
$$

moves using:

$$
-5
$$

Example:

$$
\times3
$$

moves using:

$$
\div3
$$

---

## Quadratic Equations

First check:

Can it factorise?

If yes:

$$
(x+a)(x+b)=0
$$

If no:

Use the quadratic formula.

---

## Quadratic Formula Checklist

Before substituting:

Write:

$$
a=
$$

$$
b=
$$

$$
c=
$$

Then:

$$
x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}
$$

Never skip the substitution step.

---

# 6. Simultaneous Equations Tips

Before starting:

Choose the easiest method.

## Use Substitution when:

One variable is already easy to isolate.

Example:

$$
x+y=8
$$

---

## Use Elimination when:

Variables can cancel.

Example:

$$
2x+y=5
$$

$$
2x-y=3
$$

---

Always find:

$$
x=\_\_
$$

and

$$
y=\_\_
$$

Finding only one variable is incomplete.

---

# 7. Inequality Tips

The biggest rule:

## Negative Multiply or Divide = Reverse the Sign

Example:

$$
-3x>9
$$

Divide by \(-3\):

$$
x<-3
$$

---

Remember:

$$
<
$$

and

$$
>
$$

do not include the number.

$$
\leq
$$

and

$$
\geq
$$

include the number.

---

# 8. Common Exam Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Skipping Working

Wrong:

$$
(2x+3)(x+4)=2x^2+11x+12
$$

Correct:

Show:

$$
2x(x)+2x(4)+3(x)+3(4)
$$

---

## Mistake 2: Losing Negative Signs

Wrong:

$$
-(x+5)= -x+5
$$

Correct:

$$
-(x+5)=-x-5
$$

---

## Mistake 3: Combining Wrong Families

Wrong:

$$
3x+2x^2=5x^3
$$

Correct:

They cannot combine.

---

## Mistake 4: Not Checking

Whenever possible, substitute your answer back.

---

# Final Exam Strategy

When you see a question:

## Step 1

Identify the topic:

* Simplification?
* Multiplication?
* Factorisation?
* Equation?
* Inequality?

---

## Step 2

Write the method.

Markers cannot award method marks if the method is invisible.

---

## Step 3

Work line by line.

One correct step can still earn marks even if the final answer is wrong.

---

## Step 4

Check:

* Signs
* Powers
* Brackets
* Final answer format

---

# Final Reminder

In algebra, **accuracy comes from structure**.

Do not try to do everything mentally.

Use:

$$
\boxed{\text{Identify → Method → Working → Simplify → Answer}}
$$

A clear method earns marks. A missing method loses marks, even when the final answer is correct.
